# Source and dependency notices

This is a sanitized public edition of the owner's Mount Olympus / Luminax platform. Existing first-party service, UI, Windows, and authoring-command owners are preserved. Personal registry entries and deployment settings are replaced with public examples. Generated mirrors and runtime output are not independent source owners and are excluded from the source release.

The root MIT license covers the original source and authored example artwork published here. Existing product names and artwork are example branding; they are not requirements for a contributor's installation. Six PNG assets were visually reviewed and checked for metadata before inclusion. Their exact bytes are guarded by the publication check.

Python, Node, Go, .NET, container, database, browser, audio, model, and game-engine dependencies are installed separately. This repository does not vendor their dependency directories, compiled desktop output, SDKs, model weights, or third-party game content. Keep the applicable dependency notices when distributing a compiled build. The PAPR source uses a lock-based Eleventy build; generated pages are excluded from source releases.

The optional services refer to third-party products such as Discord, Ollama, model hubs, browser engines, and game/development APIs. Their presence in source does not provide an account, model license, service entitlement, provider credit, or supported live integration. Choose and configure your own dependencies through their legitimate distributions.

The separately published Zeus project retains its own upstream MIT attribution. It is linked rather than copied into another council controller here.
